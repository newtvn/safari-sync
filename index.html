Connecting East Africa

Safari Sync is on a mission to make cross-border travel safe, affordable, and seamless for everyone.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Safari Sync emerged from a simple observation: traveling between East African countries by bus was unnecessarily complicated. Fragmented operators, unreliable schedules, and lack of transparency made what should be simple journeys into stressful experiences.

We built Safari Sync to change that. By partnering with verified bus operators across Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, and South Sudan, we've created a unified platform that makes booking and tracking cross-border bus travel as easy as ordering a ride in your city.

Safety First

Every operator is vetted. Every bus is tracked. Every journey is monitored.

Community

We're building more than a platform—we're connecting communities across borders.

Reliability

Real-time tracking, accurate ETAs, and transparent communication at every step.
